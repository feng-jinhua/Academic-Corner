Major Contributions

  • Proposed the value–price equation

    Derives the value–price equation from the law of value

    \[ \lambda_i = \frac{p_i}{\sum_{j=1}^{n} p_j q_j} L \]

    This equation can explain and resolve a variety of theoretical and practical problems in economic theory. In particular, it solves many long-standing puzzles in traditional political economy and provides rational suggestions for economic policies.

  • Establishes the value foundation of general equilibrium theory

    Assuming the aggregate price equals aggregate value and total average profit equals total surplus value, there exists exactly one set of general equilibrium prices equal to the corresponding values. This implies that general equilibrium theory can be fully grounded in the labour theory of value.

  • Proves that value transformation is a pseudo-problem

    If the total production price of all commodities equals their total value, then the production price of any single commodity is equal to its value.

  • Based on Marx’s reproduction model, proposes feasible policies combining government regulation and market mechanism to promote consumption and growth

    Within Marx’s reproduction model, letting Department I accumulate first with a constant accumulation rate (government regulation), while Department II adjusts endogenously to maintain supply-demand balance between the two departments (market mechanism), arbitrary target rates of aggregate consumption and aggregate growth can be achieved.

  • Propose the concept and calculation formula of a new GDP

    \[GDP^{*}_{t} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{it} q_{it}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{it} q_{i\tau} \big/ \sum_{i=1}^{n} p_{i\tau} q_{i\tau}}\]

    This formula eliminates the effects of inflation or deflation while fully accounting for changes in the relative prices of commodities, thereby overcoming the shortcomings of nominal and real GDP in traditional national income accounting.
